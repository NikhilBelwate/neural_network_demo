1

A relay race. The input layer passes numbers to Hidden 1, Hidden 1 passes its results to Hidden 2, and so on until the output layer.

2

Building up ideas. Early layers spot simple patterns; later layers combine them into complex ones, just like letters → words → sentences.

3

Many answers at once. These problems have several output neurons, for example 4 bits of a binary number, all learned together.
