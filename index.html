New here? How a neural network learns, in 4 steps Click to hide
1

Guess. Numbers flow left to right. Each neuron multiplies, adds and squashes, and the last layer gives an answer.

2

Measure the mistake. Compare the answer with the right one. The difference is the loss.

3

Share the blame. The error travels backwards so each weight learns how much it caused.

4

Adjust & repeat. Every weight moves a tiny step to reduce the error. Do this thousands of times.
