How does an AI like ChatGPT or Claude read your sentence? Click to hide
1

Tokenize. Chop the text into small pieces called tokens and swap each one for an ID number (steps 1–5).

2

Understand. A transformer turns IDs into "meaning vectors", lets the tokens look at each other (attention) and processes them with a neural network (steps 6–9).

3

Predict. Score every word it knows for "what comes next?", pick one, add it to the text and repeat. That is how AI writes (step 10).

⚑ This is a tiny toy model (8 numbers per token, about 670 vocabulary tokens, one transformer block). The steps are the real ones; the numbers are small enough to see.
